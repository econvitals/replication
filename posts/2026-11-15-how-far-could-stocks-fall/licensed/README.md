# Licensed inputs (not included)

These files are licensed or come from websites whose terms do not allow them to be passed on, so they are not in this package. Each is described here so that a reader with access can rebuild the rest of the post: put the file in this folder under the name given, with the columns given, and run `python run_all.py` again. Every row of `expected.csv` names the file it needs in its `needs` column.

| File | What the code expects | Where the post’s came from | Used for |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| `forward_pe_monthly.csv` | `month` (YYYY-MM), `fpe`: the S&P 500’s price over analysts’ expected earnings for the next 12 months, monthly, January 1979 to November 2026 | the Federal Reserve Board’s Financial Stability Report data tables (I/B/E/S data from Refinitiv/LSEG) from 1988 — the November 2018 edition for 1988–89, the May 2026 edition from 1990 — read monthly; 1979–87 read from Yardeni Research’s chart (archive.yardeni.com/pub/stockmktperatio.pdf, Figure 9); FactSet *Earnings Insight* from May 2026 (18.3 on November 13) | Figure 1; the forward-ratio rows of Tables 2 and 3; 18.3, the 77th percentile, 15.0; the low end of the 18-to-41 range |
| `forward_pe_on_dates.csv` | `date`, `fpe`: the forward ratio on 1996-12-05, 2000-03-24 and 2026-11-13 | 15.9 for December 5, 1996 as printed in the post (its source was not recorded; the monthly series reads 15.97); 24.4 for March 24, 2000 from FactSet *Earnings Insight* (January 24, 2020); 18.3 from FactSet (November 13, 2026) | Table 3’s forward rows; Table 1’s S&P 500 forward ratio in 2000 |
| `vti_daily.csv` | `date`, `close`: Vanguard Total Stock Market ETF (VTI) daily closes, at least September 30 to November 13, 2026 | Nasdaq historical quotes | extends the whole market past French’s September 30 data: today’s excess-growth row, the August 13 record and “about 3 percent below” |
| `sp500_daily.csv` | `date`, `close`: S&P 500 daily closes, 1946 to November 13, 2026 | S&P Dow Jones Indices (read from Yahoo Finance, ^GSPC) | Table 4’s history column; the 5 percent in Figure 4; the S&P 500 up about 135 percent since 2019 and its October 6 record; Table 1’s 31.7 |
| `spx_options_2026-11-13.json` | Cboe’s delayed-quote option chain for SPX as of the November 13, 2026 close, in Cboe’s JSON format (`data.current_price`, `data.options[]` with `option`, `bid`, `ask`) | cdn.cboe.com/api/global/delayed_quotes/options/_SPX.json, fetched November 14, 2026 | Table 4’s option-implied probabilities |
| `ai7_market_values.csv` | `company`, `market_value_usd_bn`: the seven AI companies’ market values at the November 13 close, plus a row `All listed U.S. stocks` | stockanalysis.com for the seven (together about $18.0 trillion); the working notes record them as roughly 22 percent of the whole market, but the whole market’s value used was not saved | the 11 percent and “about a fifth of the market” |
| `table1_inputs.csv` | `company`, `date`, `price`, `trailing_eps`, `market_value_usd_bn`, `trailing_pe`, `forward_pe` | today: stockanalysis.com statistics pages at the November 13 close (price, GAAP diluted earnings per share over the last four quarters, forward ratio) and multpl.com for the S&P 500’s trailing ratio (25.8, as-reported earnings); 2000: Goldman Sachs Global Investment Research, “Why we are not in a bubble… yet” (October 8, 2025), Exhibit 7 (market values and 24-month forward ratios on March 24, 2000) | Table 1 (the 2000 trailing ratios divide Goldman’s market values by the net income in `data/sec_2000_net_income.csv`) |
| `tsla_daily.csv` | `date`, `close`: Tesla daily closes, split-adjusted, April 2017 to November 13, 2026 | Nasdaq historical quotes | “up about 1,800 percent” and “about 96 percent” (with the SEC share counts in `data/`) |
| `igv_daily.csv` | `date`, `close`: iShares Expanded Tech-Software Sector ETF (IGV) daily closes, 2025 to November 13, 2026 | Yahoo Finance | software stocks down 14 percent since September 2025 |

The files in this folder other than this README are ignored by git, so a reader’s licensed data cannot be committed by accident.
